# Ore Yield

**Ore Yield** completely reimagines Minecraft's mining experience with a configurable probability-based system for stone-like blocks.

Instead of searching for visible ore veins, you'll obtain ores by mining normal stone-like blocks. Every eligible block mined has an independent chance to drop one or more ores, creating a fresh mining experience while giving pack makers and server owners complete control over resource distribution.

Supports **Forge 1.20.1**, **Fabric 1.20.1, 1.21.1, 1.21.11, 26.1.2, and 26.2**, and **NeoForge 1.21.1, 1.21.11, 26.1.2, and 26.2**.

---

## How It Works

1. Mine a supported host block (Stone, Deepslate, Tuff, Netherrack, and more).
2. Every configured ore rolls independently against its configured drop chance.
3. Successful rolls add their corresponding drops to the loot table.
4. Fortune enchantments are applied automatically where appropriate.
5. Experience is awarded for successful ore drops.
6. Explore the End for a few surprises...

---

## Features

* Replaces vanilla ore generation with a probability-based mining system.
* Highly configurable ore chances, drop counts, dimensions, Y-levels, and host blocks.
* Optional removal of vanilla ore generation.
* Built-in **Bad Luck Eliminator** to prevent extremely long unlucky streaks.
* Full Fortune enchantment support.
* Configurable experience rewards.
* Support for custom ores through the configuration file.
* Automatic detection of modded ores (optional).
* Curated compatibility for several popular content mods.
* Forge and NeoForge config screen support, plus Fabric support through Mod Menu.
* **Stone Generator** with configurable cooldown, dimension-aware output, and anti-automation controls.
* Optional **Rare Mineral Pockets** that award configurable multi-resource bonus drops.
* In-game validation, searchable ore configuration, and an Alt+F12 diagnostics report.
* Lightweight and designed to integrate naturally into vanilla survival gameplay.

## Configuration

Nearly every aspect of Ore Yield can be customized:

* Enable or disable individual ores.
* Configure drop chances and stack sizes.
* Restrict ores to specific dimensions.
* Configure Y-level ranges and peak distribution.
* Choose which blocks can produce each ore.
* Require specific pickaxe tiers.
* Add entirely new ores without writing any code.
* Configure Stone Generator output, cooldown, and harvesting safeguards.
* Enable and tune Rare Mineral Pocket categories, weights, and reward amounts.

Whether you're building a modpack, running a server, or just tweaking your single-player world, Ore Yield is designed to be flexible.
